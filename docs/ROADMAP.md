# Roadmap: features still to build

This comes from research into what Tarteel offers, based on its public help centre, blog and store listings. We build the same functionality in Tilawa's own design. We do not copy Tarteel's name, artwork or text.

## Already in Tilawa (Premium in Tarteel)
- Mistake detection while you recite: wrong, skipped and extra words
- Hidden verses (Memorize) and peeking
- Mistake history and the words you miss most
- Follow-along, with the page turning by itself when you finish it
- Voice search, listening to reciters, translations and transliteration
- Streak, daily goals and a khatm planner

## Next, in order
1. **Test mode**: start at a random place in the surahs you mark as memorized, show 1–2 cue words, then score with the existing matcher. (M)
2. **Memorize / Review / Recite goals**: a portion and a deadline, and the target recalculates if you fall behind. Memorize and Review only count while the verses are hidden. (M)
3. **Similar verses (mutashabihat)**: build an index from quran.json ahead of time, show it in the verse menu and highlight the differing words. (M)
4. **Download audio by surah or juz** for offline listening. (S–M)
5. **Tajweed colours** from openly licensed tajweed-annotated text (licence to be checked). Display only. (M)
6. **Badges and milestones**, plus a Ramadan khatm preset. (S)
7. **Tafsir** from one openly licensed source. (M)
8. **Word-by-word highlighting while audio plays**, for reciters with open timing data. (M–L)
9. **Shareable progress card or friend-challenge code**: a stand-in for groups that needs no server. (S)
10. **Yearly activity heatmap** and a toggle to hide verse markers. (S)

## Not possible for us
- **Judging tajweed or pronunciation.** The phone's speech recogniser returns words, not sounds. Tarteel does not offer this either. The closest honest alternative is tajweed colours plus "listen and compare" (play the reciter, then your own recording).
